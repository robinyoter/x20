Earlier map (2025) vs. new map (Jan–Aug 2026). Source for rates: @HEAVY_SLAYER maps from CDC WONDER + Census Vintage 2025.

Caveats. (1) The 2026 figures cover only Jan–Aug and rely on provisional CDC WONDER births, so small states are noisy (e.g. South Dakota 2.06 vs 1.95 before; Idaho 1.94). (2) Weights are each state’s total population (World Population Review 2026 projection of Census estimates), not NH-White population, so CA/TX/NY/FL are somewhat overweighted. (3) Weighted national check: 1.586 vs. the map’s stated 1.60. (4) DC (0.76) is a small-N outlier. (5) State labels were read off the map images by eye; ±0.01 transcription error is possible.
