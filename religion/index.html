How the numbers were built

  • 2020 · census. The U.S. Religion Census 2020 county file (ARDA: 3,143 counties, 372 groups), summed to states. Adherents include members, their children and regular attenders, so they run higher than official membership.
  • Regions. The four Census Bureau regions and nine divisions; each value is the sum of its states.
  • ≈2025 · estimate. For groups with recent statistics, 2020 figures are multiplied by the group's national change factor (2020 → latest report), so the distribution across states is kept.
  • UMC and Global Methodist. 7,631 churches left the UMC in 2019–2023. The share that left is estimated for each state from annual conference tallies (Lewis Center, UM News); 57% of departures are assigned to the Global Methodist Church (4,342 US churches), the rest to independents.
  • Latter-day Saints. Official Church figures as of Dec 31, 2025 for six states; other states scaled from 2020.
  • Groups without 2020 adherent counts. About 150 groups (ACNA, EFCA, UPCI, EPC and others) reported only congregations. Their national size is spread across states in proportion to congregations.

Limitations

  • Catholics, Muslims, non-denominational churches and most groups without newer data are kept at 2020 levels.
  • Historically Black churches (National Baptist and others) are undercounted in the census; their own claims are 2–4 times higher.
  • About 60 small ARDA codes (mostly Amish and Mennonite bodies) could not be confirmed against the 2020 codebook; they are marked ?.
  • Rates per 1,000 residents use 2020 census population for both periods.
